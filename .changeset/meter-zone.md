- Added: Meter says the zone out loud. Outside the best zone, screen readers hear "92%, high", "12%, low" or "45%, medium", so the warning the bar's color gives isn't only visual. The words come from the new `meterZone` label (English and Indonesian built in). A meter with `formatValue` reads your words as they are (idea 206).
