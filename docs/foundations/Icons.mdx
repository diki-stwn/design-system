## Gallery

The icons the shipped font holds, grouped by use; they work with `<Icon name="…" />` and the components' `icon` props. For a name not here, load `serupa-ds/fonts/icons-full.css` after the tokens and any name in [the full set](https://fonts.google.com/icons?icon.style=Rounded&icon.set=Material+Symbols) works. Your custom icons appear at the end once you add some.
