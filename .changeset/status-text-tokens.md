- Added: `success-text`, `warning-text` and `info-text` color tokens, with `text-success-text`, `text-warning-text` and `text-info-text` classes. Each one keeps 4.5:1 or better on `surface`, `surface-muted` and its own `*-subtle` tint in all six themes, like `danger-text`. Use them for status text: `success` (3.30:1) and `warning` (3.19:1) fail AA as text on light surfaces, so they're for icons and fills.
